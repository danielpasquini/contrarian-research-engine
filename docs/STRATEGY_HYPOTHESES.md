# Quantitative & Forensic Strategy Hypotheses — Mathematical Specification

## 1. Epistemic Framework & Doubt-Driven Principles

Every signal generated by the Contrarian Research Engine is an **exploratory quantitative ranking**, not a calibrated probability of positive return (`AGENTS.md` §5, `C-BACK-06`). Before any candidate reaches the Stage 2 LLM pipeline or Stage 3 Risk Engine, it must survive deterministic falsification:

1. **Two-Step Filter First, LLM Second (`C-ARCH-02`, `C-SCAN-01`):**
   - **Stage 1A (Daily Shock & Liquidity Filter):** Pure Python (`numpy`/`pandas`) scans the ~500-stock universe on daily bars down to the top $N_{\text{shock}} \le 25$ shock candidates.
   - **Stage 1B (Intraday 15m Smart-Money Absorption Filter):** Pure Python fetches/processes 15-minute regular-session bars (`09:30–16:00 ET`) **only** for the $\le 25$ Stage 1A candidates, confirming late-day institutional accumulation (`15:00–16:00 ET`) relative to morning retail panic (`09:30–10:30 ET`) and capping promotion to `MAX_DAILY_LLM_CANDIDATES = 15` before a single Gemini API call is made.
2. **Strict Anti-Look-Ahead & Truncation Invariance (`C-PIT-03`, `C-SCAN-07`):** Every indicator, rolling mean/variance, Wilder state, 15m intraday window metric, and SEC Form 4 cluster score computed at decision timestamp $T_{\text{dec}}$ (session $t$ market close `16:00:00 ET`) uses strictly records satisfying:
   $$\text{available\_at} \le T_{\text{dec}}$$
   Computing indicators on history truncated at $T_{\text{dec}}$ vs. full history $[1, \dots, T_{\max}]$ sliced at $T_{\text{dec}}$ must match within $10^{-12}$.
3. **Dual Price-Basis Discipline (`C-PIT-12`):**
   - **As-Of Corporate-Action-Adjusted Basis ($P^{\text{adj}}_{i,\tau \mid t}$):** Adjusted *only* for splits and cash dividends with ex-date $\tau_{\text{ex}} \le t$ (never future splits after $t$). Used for multi-day returns ($r_{1d}, r_{3d}, r_{5d}$), sector-relative return z-scores, $\text{RSI}_2$, and moving-average ratios ($\text{SMA}_{20}, \text{SMA}_{50}, \text{SMA}_{200}$).
   - **As-Of Raw Trading Basis ($P^{\text{raw}}_{i,\tau}$):** Actual unadjusted execution prices and volumes on session $\tau$. Used for the $\$10.00$ price floor, $\$20\text{M}$ 20-day average daily dollar volume ($\text{ADDV}_{20}$), 15m intraday VWAPs, and dollar $\text{ATR}_{14}$ for stop-loss placement and share sizing.
4. **Missing-Data Discipline (`C-PIT-14`):** Missing daily/15m bars, stale or missing fundamentals, missing Form 4 XML fields, or `"unverified"` LLM claims are **never** imputed with current/future values or neutral midpoints that inflate scores. Missing Stage 1 bars reject the candidate; missing Stage 2 inputs explicitly penalize confidence $C_e \in [0, 1]$ or trigger a hard `VETO`.

## 2. Stage 1: Two-Step Technical & Intraday Absorption Filter (`src/scanner/`)

### 2.1 Step 1A: Daily Shock, Liquidity & Volatility Filter ($\sim 500 \to \le 25$ Candidates)

#### 2.1.1 Tradability & Liquidity Gate (`C-SCAN-02`)
Evaluated on raw unadjusted prices $P^{\text{raw}}$ and volumes $V^{\text{raw}}$ over the trailing 20 trading sessions $\{t-19, \dots, t\}$ (requiring at least $W_{\min} = 65$ contiguous daily bars for Setup A and $200$ bars for Setup B, with zero missing sessions in $\{t-19, \dots, t\}$):

$$\text{Close}^{\text{raw}}_{i,t} \ge \$10.00$$

$$\text{ADDV}_{20,i,t} = \frac{1}{20} \sum_{k=0}^{19} \left(\text{Close}^{\text{raw}}_{i,t-k} \cdot \text{Volume}^{\text{raw}}_{i,t-k}\right) \ge \$20,000,000$$

$$\min_{k \in \{0,\dots,19\}} \text{Volume}^{\text{raw}}_{i,t-k} > 0, \qquad \text{High}^{\text{raw}}_{i,t} > \text{Low}^{\text{raw}}_{i,t}$$

#### 2.1.2 Volatility Computation: Wilder's $\text{ATR}_{14}$ (`C-SCAN-04`, `C-RISK-04`)
Computed on the as-of split-consistent raw dollar basis of session $t$:
1. **True Range ($\text{TR}_{i,\tau}$):**
   $$\text{TR}_{i,\tau} = \max\left(\text{High}_{i,\tau} - \text{Low}_{i,\tau}, \; \left|\text{High}_{i,\tau} - \text{Close}_{i,\tau-1}\right|, \; \left|\text{Low}_{i,\tau} - \text{Close}_{i,\tau-1}\right|\right)$$
2. **Wilder's Recursive Smoothing ($\alpha = \frac{1}{14}$):**
   $$\text{ATR}_{14, i, 14} = \frac{1}{14} \sum_{\tau=1}^{14} \text{TR}_{i,\tau}, \qquad \text{ATR}_{14, i, \tau} = \frac{13}{14}\,\text{ATR}_{14, i, \tau-1} + \frac{1}{14}\,\text{TR}_{i,\tau} > 0 \quad (\tau > 14)$$

#### 2.1.3 Sector/SPY-Adjusted Return Z-Scores & Exhaustion Indicators
Let $P_{i,\tau} \equiv P^{\text{adj}}_{i,\tau \mid t}$ denote the as-of adjusted close of stock $i$ and $P_{M,\tau} \equiv P^{\text{adj}}_{M,\tau \mid t}$ denote the stock's primary GICS Sector ETF (or `SPY` market benchmark).
1. **Multi-Horizon Log Returns ($h \in \{1, 3, 5\}$ sessions):**
   $$r_{h, i, \tau} = \ln\!\left(\frac{P_{i,\tau}}{P_{i,\tau - h}}\right), \qquad r_{h, M, \tau} = \ln\!\left(\frac{P_{M,\tau}}{P_{M,\tau - h}}\right)$$
2. **Past-Only Rolling Beta & Sector-Adjusted Residual Return ($W \in [60, 252]$ sessions):**
   Using strictly trailing window $\mathcal{W}_{t-1} = \{t - W, \dots, t - 1\}$ (so day-$t$'s shock cannot distort its own beta):
   $$\beta_{i, M, t-1} = \text{clip}\!\left(\frac{\sum_{\tau \in \mathcal{W}_{t-1}} (r_{1, i, \tau} - \bar{r}_{1, i})(r_{1, M, \tau} - \bar{r}_{1, M})}{\max\!\left(\sum_{\tau \in \mathcal{W}_{t-1}} (r_{1, M, \tau} - \bar{r}_{1, M})^2, \; 10^{-12}\right)}, \; 0.20, \; 3.00\right)$$
   Define the $h$-day idiosyncratic (sector-relative) residual return:
   $$e_{h, i, \tau} = r_{h, i, \tau} - \beta_{i, M, t-1}\,r_{h, M, \tau}$$
3. **Past-Only Z-Scores ($ddof = 1$ over $\mathcal{W}_t = \{t - W + 1, \dots, t\}$, $W = \min(\text{bars} - h, 252) \ge 60$):**
   $$Z\!\left(\text{Ret}_{h, i, t}\right) = \frac{r_{h, i, t} - \mu(r_{h, i})}{\max\!\left(\sigma(r_{h, i}), \; 10^{-8}\right)}, \qquad Z^{\text{rel}}_{h, i, t} = \frac{e_{h, i, t} - \mu(e_{h, i})}{\max\!\left(\sigma(e_{h, i}), \; 10^{-8}\right)}$$
   Define the effective shock z-score for horizon $h \in \{1, 3, 5\}$:
   $$Z^{\text{eff}}_{h, i, t} = \min\!\left(Z\!\left(\text{Ret}_{h, i, t}\right), \; Z^{\text{rel}}_{h, i, t}\right), \qquad Z_{\text{shock}, i, t} = \min_{h \in \{1, 3, 5\}} Z^{\text{eff}}_{h, i, t}$$
4. **Wilder's $\text{RSI}_2$ ($\alpha_{\text{RSI}} = 0.5$) & Daily Intraday Recovery Ratio ($\text{IRR}_{i,t}$):**
   $$\overline{U}_{\tau} = 0.5\,\overline{U}_{\tau-1} + 0.5\,\max(\Delta P_{\tau}, 0), \qquad \overline{D}_{\tau} = 0.5\,\overline{D}_{\tau-1} + 0.5\,\max(-\Delta P_{\tau}, 0)$$
   $$\text{RSI}_{2, i, t} = 100.0 - \frac{100.0}{1.0 + (\overline{U}_t / \overline{D}_t)} \in [0, 100], \qquad \text{IRR}_{i, t} = \frac{\text{Close}^{\text{raw}}_{i, t} - \text{Low}^{\text{raw}}_{i, t}}{\text{High}^{\text{raw}}_{i, t} - \text{Low}^{\text{raw}}_{i, t}} \in [0, 1]$$
5. **Step 1A Qualification Rule (Top $\le 25$ Daily Shock Shortlist):**
   A liquid ticker qualifies for Step 1B 15-minute bar inspection iff **Setup A (Daily Capitulation)** OR **Setup B (Structural Uptrend Pullback)** holds:
   - **Setup A:** $Z_{\text{shock}, i, t} \le -2.0 \;\land\; \text{RSI}_{2, i, t} \le 10.0 \;\land\; \left(\text{IRR}_{i, t} \ge 0.35 \;\lor\; \left[\text{VolRatio}_{i, t-1} \ge 2.0 \land V_{i,t} < V_{i,t-1} \land \text{Low}_{i,t} \ge \text{Low}_{i,t-1} - 0.25\,\text{ATR}_{14,i,t}\right]\right)$
   - **Setup B:** $P_{i, t} > \text{SMA}_{200, i, t} \;\land\; \text{SMA}_{50, i, t} > \text{SMA}_{200, i, t} \;\land\; Z_{\text{shock}, i, t} \le -1.5 \;\land\; \text{RSI}_{2, i, t} \le 15.0 \;\land\; \min(|P_{i,t} - \text{SMA}_{50}|, |P_{i,t} - \text{SMA}_{200}|) \le 1.0\,\text{ATR}_{14, i, t} \;\land\; \text{IRR}_{i, t} \ge 0.30$
   - Qualifying tickers are sorted by $(-Z_{\text{shock}, i, t} \text{ severity}, \text{ticker})$ and capped at $N_{\text{shock}} = 25$ for Step 1B 15-minute bar retrieval.

### 2.2 Step 1B: Intraday Smart-Money Absorption Filter (Alpaca 15m Bars, $\le 25 \to \le 15$ Candidates)

#### 2.2.1 Microstructure Hypothesis (`forensic-alpha` §4)
Retail panic orders, margin calls, and overnight stop-loss sweeps cluster disproportionately in the **AM Retail Panic Window (`09:30–10:30 ET`)**, whereas institutional execution desks (TWAP/VWAP accumulation and closing-bell liquidity absorption) concentrate in the **PM Institutional Accumulation Window (`15:00–16:00 ET`)**. A genuine contrarian bottom exhibits early sell-side exhaustion followed by late-session institutional absorption that holds above the morning panic low and reclaims the afternoon/session VWAP. Conversely, stocks where `15:00–16:00 ET` exhibits accelerating negative flow and new session lows are undergoing institutional distribution ("falling knives") and must be rejected.

#### 2.2.2 Intraday 15-Minute Bar Partition & Point-in-Time Definitions
For a standard regular trading session (`09:30:00 ET` to `16:00:00 ET`), let $b \in \{1, 2, \dots, 26\}$ index the twenty-six 15-minute OHLCV bars $(O_b, H_b, L_b, C_b, V_b, \text{VWAP}_b)$ with bar close timestamps $\le T_{\text{dec}}$.
*(If bar-level $\text{VWAP}_b$ is not provided by the feed, define typical price $\text{TP}_b = \frac{H_b + L_b + C_b}{3}$).*

Partition the session into:
- **AM Retail Panic Window ($\mathcal{B}_{\text{AM}} = \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$, `09:30–10:30 ET`):**
  $$O_{\text{AM}} = O_1, \quad C_{\text{AM}} = C_4, \quad H_{\text{AM}} = \max_{b \in \mathcal{B}_{\text{AM}}} H_b, \quad L_{\text{AM}} = \min_{b \in \mathcal{B}_{\text{AM}}} L_b, \quad V_{\text{AM}} = \sum_{b \in \mathcal{B}_{\text{AM}}} V_b$$
  $$\text{VWAP}_{\text{AM}} = \frac{\sum_{b \in \mathcal{B}_{\text{AM}}} \text{TP}_b \cdot V_b}{\max(V_{\text{AM}}, 1)}$$
- **PM Institutional Accumulation Window ($\mathcal{B}_{\text{PM}} = \{23, 24, 25, 26\}$, `15:00–16:00 ET`):**
  $$O_{\text{PM}} = O_{23}, \quad C_{\text{PM}} = C_{26}, \quad H_{\text{PM}} = \max_{b \in \mathcal{B}_{\text{PM}}} H_b, \quad L_{\text{PM}} = \min_{b \in \mathcal{B}_{\text{PM}}} L_b, \quad V_{\text{PM}} = \sum_{b \in \mathcal{B}_{\text{PM}}} V_b$$
  $$\text{VWAP}_{\text{PM}} = \frac{\sum_{b \in \mathcal{B}_{\text{PM}}} \text{TP}_b \cdot V_b}{\max(V_{\text{PM}}, 1)}, \qquad \text{VWAP}_{\text{day}} = \frac{\sum_{b=1}^{26} \text{TP}_b \cdot V_b}{\max\!\left(\sum_{b=1}^{26} V_b, 1\right)}$$

#### 2.2.3 Exact Intraday Absorption Formulas
1. **ATR-Normalized Window Returns ($R_{\text{AM}, i, t}$ and $R_{\text{PM}, i, t}$):**
   Normalize price changes by daily $\text{ATR}_{14, i, t}$ so cross-sectional comparisons are volatility-invariant:
   $$R_{\text{AM}, i, t} = \frac{C_{\text{AM}, i, t} - \text{Close}^{\text{raw}}_{i, t-1}}{\text{ATR}_{14, i, t}}, \qquad R_{\text{PM}, i, t} = \frac{C_{\text{PM}, i, t} - O_{\text{PM}, i, t}}{\text{ATR}_{14, i, t}}$$
   *(Note: $R_{\text{AM}, i, t}$ captures both the overnight gap and the first hour of panic selling from prior close $\text{Close}^{\text{raw}}_{i, t-1}$ to `10:30 ET`; intra-hour AM return is $R^{\text{intra}}_{\text{AM}, i, t} = \frac{C_{\text{AM}, i, t} - O_{\text{AM}, i, t}}{\text{ATR}_{14, i, t}}$).*

2. **PM-vs-AM Return Divergence ($\Delta R_{\text{PM-AM}, i, t}$):**
   $$\Delta R_{\text{PM-AM}, i, t} = R_{\text{PM}, i, t} - R_{\text{AM}, i, t}$$
   When retail panics in the morning ($R_{\text{AM}, i, t} < 0$) and institutions accumulate in the final hour ($R_{\text{PM}, i, t} \ge 0$), $\Delta R_{\text{PM-AM}, i, t} > 0$.

3. **Signed Institutional Volume Absorption Ratio ($\text{SMAR}_{i, t} \in [-1.0, 1.0]$):**
   For each 15-minute bar $b$, define the bar's close-location signed volume $SV_b = V_b \cdot \frac{2 C_b - H_b - L_b}{\max(H_b - L_b, \; 0.01)}$ (which lies in $[-V_b, +V_b]$, equal to $+V_b$ when bar closes on its high and $-V_b$ on its low).
   Define the window net signed volume ratios:
   $$\text{Flow}_{\text{AM}, i, t} = \frac{\sum_{b \in \mathcal{B}_{\text{AM}}} SV_b}{\max(V_{\text{AM}, i, t}, 1)} \in [-1, 1], \qquad \text{Flow}_{\text{PM}, i, t} = \frac{\sum_{b \in \mathcal{B}_{\text{PM}}} SV_b}{\max(V_{\text{PM}, i, t}, 1)} \in [-1, 1]$$
   Define the **Smart-Money Absorption Ratio ($\text{SMAR}_{i, t} \in [0, 100]$)**:
   $$\text{SMAR}_{i, t} = 100 \cdot \text{clip}\!\left(0.40\,\text{clip}\!\left(\frac{\Delta R_{\text{PM-AM}, i, t}}{1.50}, 0, 1\right) + 0.35\,\text{clip}\!\left(\frac{\text{Flow}_{\text{PM}, i, t} + 0.20}{0.80}, 0, 1\right) + 0.25\,\mathbb{I}\!\left(C_{\text{PM}, i, t} \ge \text{VWAP}_{\text{PM}, i, t}\right), \; 0.0, \; 1.0\right)$$

#### 2.2.4 Mandatory Step 1B Intraday Confirmation Gate (`C-SCAN-05`)
To promote a daily shock candidate from Step 1A to the Stage 2 LLM pipeline (`passes_stage1 = True`), all four intraday absorption conditions must hold:
1. **No Late-Session Cliff / Low Undercut:** The PM window (`15:00–16:00 ET`) must hold above the AM panic low within $0.10\,\text{ATR}_{14, i, t}$ (and for strict panic reversals, $L_{\text{PM}, i, t} \ge L_{\text{AM}, i, t}$):
   $$L_{\text{PM}, i, t} \ge L_{\text{AM}, i, t} - 0.10 \cdot \text{ATR}_{14, i, t}$$
2. **Positive PM-vs-AM Divergence & Non-Negative PM Accumulation:**
   $$\Delta R_{\text{PM-AM}, i, t} > 0.0 \quad \land \quad R_{\text{PM}, i, t} \ge -0.05 \quad \land \quad \text{Flow}_{\text{PM}, i, t} \ge 0.0$$
3. **Late-Session VWAP Support / Reclaim:**
   $$C_{\text{PM}, i, t} \ge \text{VWAP}_{\text{PM}, i, t} \quad \lor \quad C_{\text{PM}, i, t} \ge \text{VWAP}_{\text{day}, i, t}$$
4. **Minimum Smart-Money Absorption Score:**
   $$\text{SMAR}_{i, t} \ge 50.0$$

- **Missing 15m Bars / Early-Close Handling (`C-PIT-14`):** If any bar in $\mathcal{B}_{\text{AM}}$ or $\mathcal{B}_{\text{PM}}$ is missing or has zero volume (or on NYSE half-day sessions closing at `13:00 ET` where `15:00–16:00 ET` does not exist), Step 1B cannot verify institutional absorption and fails closed (`passes_stage1 = False`), unless explicitly running in daily-only historical fallback mode where missing 15m data applies a $0.75\times$ confidence penalty to Stage 2.
- **Stage 1 Priority Score & Cap (`C-SCAN-06`):**
  $$S_{\text{stage1}, i, t} = 0.40\,\text{clip}\!\left(\frac{-Z_{\text{shock}, i, t} - 1.5}{3.5}, 0, 1\right)\cdot 100 + 0.35\,\text{SMAR}_{i, t} + 0.25\,\text{clip}\!\left(\frac{15.0 - \text{RSI}_{2, i, t}}{15.0}, 0, 1\right)\cdot 100$$
  Sorted deterministically by `(-S_stage1, ticker)` and truncated to `MAX_DAILY_LLM_CANDIDATES = 15`.

## 3. Shared Point-in-Time Conditioning Sub-Scores & Orthogonalization

To prevent collinear double-counting across engines (`C-STRAT-02`), shared technical stabilization ($T_{\text{stab}}$) and balance-sheet solvency ($F_{\text{health}}$) are defined once and excluded from each engine's **Orthogonal Core Alpha Score ($A_e$)**:

1. **Technical & Microstructure Stabilization Sub-Score ($T_{\text{stab}, i, t} \in [0, 100]$):**
   $$T_{\text{stab}, i, t} = 0.50\,\text{SMAR}_{i, t} + 0.30\,\left(100 \cdot \text{IRR}_{i, t}\right) + 0.20\,\left(100 \cdot \text{clip}\!\left(\frac{15.0 - \text{RSI}_{2, i, t}}{15.0}, 0, 1\right)\right)$$
2. **Point-in-Time Balance-Sheet Solvency Sub-Score ($F_{\text{health}, i, t} \in [0, 100]$):**
   From the latest 10-Q/10-K with $\text{available\_at} \le T_{\text{dec}}$ and age $\Delta t_{\text{filing}} \le 180$ calendar days:
   $$c_{\text{liq}} = \frac{\text{clip}(\text{Cash} / \max(\text{STDebt}, \$1), 0, 2)}{2}, \quad c_{\text{lev}} = \text{clip}\!\left(1 - \frac{\text{NetDebt}/\text{EBITDA}}{4.0}, 0, 1\right), \quad c_{\text{fcf}} = \text{clip}\!\left(\frac{\text{TTM\_FCF}/\text{TTM\_Rev} + 0.05}{0.20}, 0, 1\right)$$
   $$F_{\text{health}, i, t} = 100 \cdot \left(0.40\,c_{\text{liq}} + 0.35\,c_{\text{lev}} + 0.25\,c_{\text{fcf}}\right)$$
3. **Verified Claim Evidence Ratio ($\rho_{\text{verified}, i, t} \in [0, 1]$):**
   Claims with `source_id == "unverified"` receive weight $0.0$ (`C-LLM-04`). `TIER_1_SEC` receives weight $1.0$, `TIER_2_WIRE` $0.8$, `TIER_3_SECONDARY` $0.4$.

## 4. Stage 2 Quantitative & Forensic Engines (`src/strategies/engines.py`)

### 4.1 Engine 1: Negative Overreaction Engine (`S_overreaction`)

#### 4.1.1 Mathematical Hypothesis
When a stock suffers an extreme sector-adjusted 1-to-5 day sell-off ($Z^{\text{rel}}_{h, i, t} \le -2.0$) driven by liquidity panic or knee-jerk selling, yet verified fundamental/guidance revisions are benign ($\phi_{\text{fund}} \ge -0.25$) and balance-sheet solvency is intact ($F_{\text{health}} \ge 50$), the excess price drop relative to fundamental revision mean-reverts over 3 to 20 sessions once Step 1B confirms late-day institutional absorption.

#### 4.1.2 Required Point-in-Time Inputs ($\text{available\_at} \le T_{\text{dec}}$)
- Sector-adjusted return z-scores $Z^{\text{rel}}_{3d, i, t}, Z^{\text{rel}}_{5d, i, t}$ and standalone $Z(\text{Ret}_{3d, i, t}), Z(\text{Ret}_{5d, i, t})$.
- Step 1B Smart-Money Absorption Ratio $\text{SMAR}_{i, t}$ and $T_{\text{stab}, i, t}$.
- PIT fundamental health $F_{\text{health}, i, t}$ and optional quantitative guidance/consensus EPS revision $\Delta \text{Guid}_{i, t} \in [-1, 1]$ (percentage revision in FY1 EPS guidance from 8-K Item 7.01/2.02 if issued in trailing 5 sessions; $0.0$ if unchanged).
- Stage 2 `EventAnalysisResponse`: `fundamental_impact_score` $\phi_{\text{fund}} \in [-1, 1]$ and verified claim ratio $\rho_{\text{verified}, i, t}$.

#### 4.1.3 Exact Formulas & Past-Only Normalization
1. **Sector-Adjusted Shock Magnitude Sub-Score ($T_{\text{mag}, i, t} \in [0, 100]$):**
   Using $Z^{\text{rel}}_{\text{shock}, i, t} = \min\!\left(Z^{\text{rel}}_{3d, i, t}, Z^{\text{rel}}_{5d, i, t}, Z(\text{Ret}_{3d, i, t})\right)$:
   $$T_{\text{mag}, i, t} = 100 \cdot \text{clip}\!\left(\frac{-Z^{\text{rel}}_{\text{shock}, i, t} - 1.5}{3.0}, \; 0.0, \; 1.0\right)$$
2. **Guidance & Fundamental Revision Disproportion Sub-Score ($E_{\text{disp}, i, t} \in [0, 100]$):**
   Combine verified LLM fundamental assessment $\phi_{\text{fund}}$ with deterministic guidance revision $\Delta \text{Guid}_{i, t}$ (scaled by $10\times$ so a $-5\%$ guidance cut equals $-0.50$):
   $$\phi^{\text{eff}}_{\text{fund}, i, t} = \min\!\left(\rho_{\text{verified}, i, t} \cdot \phi_{\text{fund}, i, t} + (1 - \rho_{\text{verified}, i, t})\cdot(-0.50), \; \text{clip}(10.0 \cdot \Delta \text{Guid}_{i, t}, -1.0, 1.0)\right)$$
   If $\phi^{\text{eff}}_{\text{fund}, i, t} < -0.40$ (material fundamental or guidance deterioration > 4%), $E_{\text{disp}, i, t} = 0.0$. Otherwise:
   $$E_{\text{disp}, i, t} = 100 \cdot \text{clip}\!\left(\frac{\phi^{\text{eff}}_{\text{fund}, i, t} + 0.40}{0.60}, \; 0.0, \; 1.0\right) \cdot \text{clip}\!\left(\frac{-Z^{\text{rel}}_{\text{shock}, i, t}}{2.0}, \; 0.0, \; 1.0\right)$$
3. **Engine 1 Orthogonal Core Alpha ($A_{\text{overreaction}, i, t}$) & Standalone Score ($S_{\text{overreaction}, i, t}$):**
   $$A_{\text{overreaction}, i, t} = 0.55\,T_{\text{mag}, i, t} + 0.45\,E_{\text{disp}, i, t}$$
   $$S_{\text{overreaction}, i, t} = C_{\text{overreaction}, i, t} \cdot \left(0.35\,T_{\text{mag}, i, t} + 0.25\,E_{\text{disp}, i, t} + 0.20\,T_{\text{stab}, i, t} + 0.20\,F_{\text{health}, i, t}\right)$$

#### 4.1.4 Missing-Data & Failure Conditions
- **Missing Data (`C-PIT-14`):** Missing/stale ($>180\text{d}$) 10-Q/10-K sets $F_{\text{health}} = 0.0$ and multiplies $C_{\text{overreaction}}$ by $0.50$. Zero verified claims ($\rho_{\text{verified}} = 0$) sets $E_{\text{disp}} = 0.0$ and multiplies $C_{\text{overreaction}}$ by $0.60$.
- **Thesis Invalidation:** Pre-entry forced to $S_{\text{overreaction}} = 0.0$ if $\phi^{\text{eff}}_{\text{fund}, i, t} < -0.40$ or $F_{\text{health}, i, t} < 30.0$ (when present).

### 4.2 Engine 2: Narrative & Contagion Divergence Engine (`S_divergence`)

#### 4.2.1 Mathematical Hypothesis
Discrepancies arise via two forensic mechanisms:
1. **Primary-vs-Headline Divergence:** Secondary media portals amplify sensationalist negative headlines ($\phi_{\text{head}} \le -0.25$, high syndication redundancy $R_{\text{syn}}$, low novelty $\nu$) while primary SEC filings (`TIER_1_SEC` 8-K/10-Q/10-K) and forensic Item 1A/MD&A text-diff stability ($J_{\text{sim}} \ge 0.85$) show intact fundamentals ($\Delta \phi = \phi_{\text{fund}} - \phi_{\text{head}} > 0.20$).
2. **Cross-Entity Sympathy Contagion & Rival Failure (`forensic-alpha` §1, `C-FOR-01`):** When a sector peer $p \ne i$ suffers a severe idiosyncratic shock ($r_{1d, p, t} \le -0.08$, e.g., product recall, contract loss, regulatory halt, or guidance miss), passive sector ETFs and basket algorithms sell the entire peer group in sympathy. If target $i$ has zero direct operational impairment ($\phi_{\text{fund}, i, t} \ge 0.0$) or stands to capture market share from the failing rival (`is_sympathy_contagion == True`), the sympathy drawdown in $i$ is a high-asymmetry dislocation.

#### 4.2.2 Required Point-in-Time Inputs ($\text{available\_at} \le T_{\text{dec}}$)
- Stage 2 `EventAnalysisResponse`: $\phi_{\text{head}}, \phi_{\text{fund}}, d_{\text{llm}}, \nu$, syndication redundancy $R_{\text{syn}} = 1 - N_{\text{clusters}}/\max(N_{\text{raw}}, 1)$, `is_sympathy_contagion: bool`, and verified Tier-1/2 claim ratio $\rho_{\text{tier12}, i, t}$.
- Peer Contagion Inputs (`C-FOR-01`): Worst GICS sub-industry peer 3-day return $r_{\text{peer\_min}, i, t} = \min_{p \in \mathcal{P}(i) \setminus \{i\}} r_{3d, p, t}$, historical 60-day return correlation $\rho_{i, p, t-1}$ ($\le t-1$), and Stage 2 verification that shock originated at peer $p$ (`is_sympathy_contagion == True`).
- SEC Filing Text-Diff Forensics (`C-FOR-02`): Jaccard token similarity $J_{\text{sim}, i, t} \in [0, 1]$ on Item 1A Risk Factors between latest 10-Q/10-K and prior filing ($\text{available\_at} \le T_{\text{dec}}$), plus count of newly injected critical risk terms $N_{\text{new\_risk}, i, t}$ (`"restatement"`, `"going concern"`, `"subpoena"`, `"covenant default"`, `"material weakness"`).

#### 4.2.3 Exact Formulas & Past-Only Normalization
1. **Primary-vs-Headline Divergence Sub-Score ($D_{\text{filing}, i, t} \in [0, 100]$):**

   $$D_{\text{base}, i, t} = \text{clip}\!\left(\frac{\Delta \phi_{i, t} - 0.20}{1.20}, \; 0.0, \; 1.0\right), \qquad b_{\text{diff}, i, t} = 0.15 \cdot \mathbb{I}\!\left(J_{\text{sim}, i, t} \ge 0.85 \land N_{\text{new\_risk}, i, t} = 0\right)$$
   $$D_{\text{filing}, i, t} = 100 \cdot \rho_{\text{tier12}, i, t} \cdot \text{clip}\!\left(0.70\,D_{\text{base}, i, t} + 0.15\,d_{\text{llm}, i, t} + b_{\text{diff}, i, t}, \; 0.0, \; 1.0\right)$$

2. **Cross-Entity Rival Failure & Sympathy Contagion Bonus ($K_{\text{contagion}, i, t} \in [0, 100]$):**

   $$K_{\text{contagion}, i, t} = \begin{cases} 100 \cdot \rho_{\text{verified}, i, t} \cdot \text{clip}\!\left(0.50\,\text{clip}\!\left(\frac{-r_{\text{peer\_min}, i, t} - 0.08}{0.12}, 0, 1\right) + 0.30\,\rho_{i, p, t-1} + 0.20\,\text{clip}(\phi_{\text{fund}, i, t} + 0.20, 0, 1), \; 0, \; 1\right) & \text{if active} \\ 0.0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

3. **Media Sensationalism & Stale-Syndication Sub-Score ($M_{\text{over}, i, t} \in [0, 100]$):**

4. **Engine 2 Orthogonal Core Alpha ($A_{\text{divergence}, i, t}$) & Standalone Score ($S_{\text{divergence}, i, t}$):**

   $$A_{\text{divergence}, i, t} = \text{clip}\!\left(0.55\,D_{\text{filing}, i, t} + 0.25\,M_{\text{over}, i, t} + 0.35\,K_{\text{contagion}, i, t}, \; 0.0, \; 100.0\right)$$

### 4.3 Engine 3: Sentiment & Insider Conviction Engine (`S_inflection`)

#### 4.3.1 Mathematical Hypothesis
High-conviction multi-week reversals occur when narrative sentiment inflects upward from deeply negative territory ($\Delta \overline{\phi}_{i, t} \ge 0.25$) while corporate insiders with direct operational visibility execute **discretionary open-market Form 4 purchases** (`transactionCode == 'P'`, outside pre-scheduled Rule `10b5-1` plans) into the drawdown within the trailing 10 trading sessions (`forensic-alpha` §3, `C-FOR-03`), confirmed by price holding structural moving-average support ($\text{SMA}_{50}/\text{SMA}_{200}$).

#### 4.3.2 Required Point-in-Time Inputs ($\text{available\_at} \le T_{\text{dec}}$)
1. **SEC Form 4 XML Transactions (`C-FOR-03`):** All Form 4 filings for ticker $i$ with SEC EDGAR acceptance timestamp $\text{available\_at} \le T_{\text{dec}}$ inside the trailing 10 trading sessions $[t-9, t]$. Each transaction record $k$ parsed from XML must expose:
   - `transaction_code: str` (strictly filter for `'P'` = open-market purchase; exclude `'M'`, `'A'`, `'F'`, `'G'`, `'J'`, and flag `'S'` = open-market sale).
   - `is_10b5_1: bool` (true if `<aff10b5One>1</aff10b5One>` is checked OR footnotes match regex `(?i)10b5-1|rule\s+10b-5|pre-arranged\s+trading\s+plan`).
   - `officer_role: Literal["CEO", "CFO", "COO", "PRES", "EVP_VP", "DIRECTOR", "TEN_PCT_OWNER", "OTHER"]`.
   - `dollar_value: float` ($\text{shares}_k \times \text{price\_per\_share}_k$).
   - `insider_id: str` (CIK of reporting owner, to count distinct insiders).
2. **Trailing PIT Narrative Sentiment Window:** Event sentiment scores in $\mathcal{W}_{\text{prior}} = [t-15, t-4]$ ($\overline{\phi}_{\text{prior}, i, t}$) and $\mathcal{W}_{\text{recent}} = [t-3, t]$ ($\overline{\phi}_{\text{recent}, i, t}$).
3. **Structural Support & Intraday Absorption:** $\text{SMA}_{50, i, t}, \text{SMA}_{200, i, t}, \text{ATR}_{14, i, t}, \text{SMAR}_{i, t}$.

#### 4.3.3 Exact Formulas & Past-Only Normalization
1. **SEC Form 4 Discretionary Cluster Score ($F_{4, i, t} \in [0, 100]$, `C-FOR-03`):**
   - **Step 1 (Strict Discretionary Filter):** Retain only open-market purchases $k \in \mathcal{K}_{\text{buy}}(i, t)$ satisfying:
     $$\text{transaction\_code}_k = \text{'P'} \;\land\; \text{is\_10b5\_1}_k = \text{False} \;\land\; \text{dollar\_value}_k \ge \$25,000 \;\land\; \text{available\_at}_k \le T_{\text{dec}}$$
     Also define discretionary C-suite open-market sales $\mathcal{K}_{\text{sell}}(i, t) = \{k : \text{transaction\_code}_k = \text{'S'} \land \text{is\_10b5\_1}_k = \text{False} \land \text{officer\_role}_k \in \{\text{CEO}, \text{CFO}, \text{COO}, \text{PRES}\} \land \text{dollar\_value}_k \ge \$100,000\}$.
     If $|\mathcal{K}_{\text{sell}}(i, t)| > 0$ (discretionary C-suite dumping during drawdown), set $F_{4, i, t} = 0.0$ and trigger Engine 3 insider counter-evidence veto.
   - **Step 2 (Officer Role Weight $w_{\text{role}}(k)$ & Dollar Conviction Scaling $s_{\$}(k)$):**
     $$w_{\text{role}}(k) = \begin{cases} 1.00 & \text{if } \text{officer\_role}_k \in \{\text{"CEO"}, \text{"CFO"}\} \\ 0.80 & \text{if } \text{officer\_role}_k \in \{\text{"COO"}, \text{"PRES"}\} \\ 0.50 & \text{if } \text{officer\_role}_k \in \{\text{"EVP\_VP"}, \text{"DIRECTOR"}\} \\ 0.30 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
     $$s_{\$}(k) = \text{clip}\!\left(\frac{\ln(\text{dollar\_value}_k / \$25,000)}{\ln(\$1,000,000 / \$25,000)}, \; 0.20, \; 1.00\right)$$
     *(Maps $\$25\text{k} \mapsto 0.20$, $\$158\text{k} \mapsto 0.50$, $\ge \$1\text{M} \mapsto 1.00$).*
   - **Step 3 (Multi-Insider Cluster Multiplier $M_{\text{cluster}}$):**
     Let $N_{\text{insiders}} = \left|\{\text{insider\_id}_k : k \in \mathcal{K}_{\text{buy}}(i, t)\}\right|$ be the number of distinct insiders buying:
     $$M_{\text{cluster}} = \begin{cases} 0.00 & \text{if } N_{\text{insiders}} = 0 \\ 1.00 & \text{if } N_{\text{insiders}} = 1 \\ 1.35 & \text{if } N_{\text{insiders}} = 2 \\ 1.65 & \text{if } N_{\text{insiders}} \ge 3 \end{cases}$$
     $$F_{4, i, t} = 100 \cdot \text{clip}\!\left(M_{\text{cluster}} \cdot \max_{k \in \mathcal{K}_{\text{buy}}(i, t)}\!\left(w_{\text{role}}(k) \cdot s_{\$}(k)\right), \; 0.0, \; 1.0\right)$$
     *(Example: A single CEO discretionary purchase of $\$250\text{k}$ gives $w_{\text{role}}=1.0, s_{\$}=0.624 \implies F_4 = 62.4$. If both the CEO and CFO buy $\$250\text{k}$ ($N_{\text{insiders}}=2$), $M_{\text{cluster}}=1.35 \implies F_4 = 84.2$).*

2. **Narrative Sentiment Inflection Sub-Score ($I_{\text{inflect}, i, t} \in [0, 100]$):**
   Let $\Delta \overline{\phi}_{i, t} = \overline{\phi}_{\text{recent}, i, t} - \overline{\phi}_{\text{prior}, i, t}$.
   $$I_{\text{inflect}, i, t} = \begin{cases} 0.0 & \text{if } \overline{\phi}_{\text{prior}, i, t} > -0.25 \text{ or } \Delta \overline{\phi}_{i, t} < 0.25 \text{ or } \phi_{\text{fund}, i, t} < -0.15 \\ 100 \cdot \rho_{\text{verified}, i, t} \cdot \text{clip}\!\left(\frac{\Delta \overline{\phi}_{i, t} - 0.20}{1.00}, \; 0.0, \; 1.0\right) & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

3. **Structural Support & Intraday Absorption Sub-Score ($T_{\text{support}, i, t} \in [0, 100]$):**
   For $P_{i, t} > \text{SMA}_{200, i, t}$ and nearest support $S^{\star}_{i, t} \in \{\text{SMA}_{50, i, t}, \text{SMA}_{200, i, t}\}$ below $P_{i, t}$ with $d_{\text{ATR}, i, t} = (P_{i, t} - S^{\star}_{i, t})/\text{ATR}_{14, i, t} \ge 0$:
   $$s_{\text{prox}, i, t} = \mathbb{I}(P_{i, t} > \text{SMA}_{200, i, t}) \cdot \text{clip}\!\left(1.0 - \frac{d_{\text{ATR}, i, t}}{2.5}, \; 0.0, \; 1.0\right)$$
   $$T_{\text{support}, i, t} = 100 \cdot \text{clip}\!\left(0.45\,s_{\text{prox}, i, t} + 0.40\,\left(\frac{\text{SMAR}_{i, t}}{100.0}\right) + 0.15\,\mathbb{I}(\text{SMA}_{50, i, t} > \text{SMA}_{200, i, t}), \; 0.0, \; 1.0\right)$$

4. **Engine 3 Orthogonal Core Alpha ($A_{\text{inflection}, i, t}$) & Standalone Score ($S_{\text{inflection}, i, t}$):**
   Engine 3 can be driven by narrative inflection + support, discretionary Form 4 cluster conviction + support, or both in confluence:
   $$A_{\text{inflection}, i, t} = \text{clip}\!\left(0.50\,I_{\text{inflect}, i, t} + 0.50\,F_{4, i, t} + 0.15\,\min(I_{\text{inflect}, i, t}, F_{4, i, t}), \; 0.0, \; 100.0\right)$$
   $$S_{\text{inflection}, i, t} = C_{\text{inflection}, i, t} \cdot \text{clip}\!\left(0.55\,A_{\text{inflection}, i, t} + 0.25\,T_{\text{support}, i, t} + 0.20\,F_{\text{health}, i, t}, \; 0.0, \; 100.0\right)$$

#### 4.3.4 Missing-Data & Failure Conditions
- **Missing Data (`C-PIT-14`):**
  - If no qualifying Form 4 filings exist in $[t-9, t]$, $F_{4, i, t} = 0.0$.
  - If $\mathcal{W}_{\text{prior}}$ or $\mathcal{W}_{\text{recent}}$ lacks verified events, $I_{\text{inflect}, i, t} = 0.0$.
  - If **both** $I_{\text{inflect}, i, t} = 0.0$ and $F_{4, i, t} = 0.0$ (neither narrative inflection nor insider buying is present), $C_{\text{inflection}, i, t} = 0.0 \implies S_{\text{inflection}, i, t} = 0.0$.
  - If $< 200$ price bars exist for $\text{SMA}_{200}$, $T_{\text{support}, i, t} = 0.0$ and $C_{\text{inflection}, i, t}$ is multiplied by $0.60$.
  - If 10-Q/10-K fundamentals are missing/stale, $F_{\text{health}, i, t} = 0.0$ and $C_{\text{inflection}, i, t}$ is multiplied by $0.70$.
- **Thesis Invalidation:** Pre-entry forced to $0.0$ if discretionary C-suite selling occurs ($|\mathcal{K}_{\text{sell}}(i, t)| > 0$) or if $P_{i, t} < \text{SMA}_{200, i, t}$ when $F_{4, i, t} < 60.0$.

## 5. Ensemble Combination Rules & Mandatory Veto Gate (`src/strategies/ensemble.py`)

### 5.1 Multiplicative Safety & Veto Gate (`C-STRAT-01`, `C-STRAT-03`, `C-RISK-05`)
$$G_{\text{stage1}, i, t} = \mathbb{I}\!\left(\text{passes\_stage1}_{i, t} = \text{True}\right)$$
$$G_{\text{earnings}, i, t} = \mathbb{I}\!\left(\text{earnings\_blackout}_{i, t} = \text{False}\right)$$
$$G_{\text{llm}, i, t} = \mathbb{I}\!\left(\text{veto\_decision}_{i, t} = \text{"APPROVE"} \;\land\; \left|\text{detected\_red\_flags}_{i, t}\right| = 0\right)$$
$$G_{\text{total}, i, t} = G_{\text{stage1}, i, t} \cdot G_{\text{earnings}, i, t} \cdot G_{\text{llm}, i, t} \in \{0, 1\}$$

If $G_{\text{total}, i, t} = 0$, then $S_{\text{composite}, i, t} = 0.0$ and $\text{is\_actionable}_{i, t} = \text{False}$.

### 5.2 Orthogonalized Non-Collinear Ensemble Score (`C-STRAT-02`, `C-STRAT-04`)
1. **Winning Primary Engine ($e^{\star}$):**
   $$e^{\star} = \arg\max_{e \in \{1, 2, 3\}} S_{e, i, t}, \qquad S_{\max, i, t} = S_{e^{\star}, i, t}$$
   Because $S_{e^{\star}, i, t}$ already contains the shared technical stabilization ($T_{\text{stab}}$ / $T_{\text{support}}$) and balance-sheet health ($F_{\text{health}}$) terms once, the secondary engines must **never** re-add $T_{\text{stab}}$ or $F_{\text{health}}$.
2. **Orthogonal Core-Alpha Synergy Bonus ($B_{\text{orth}, i, t} \in [0.0, 15.0]$):**
   Using only the confidence-weighted orthogonal core alphas $\widetilde{A}_{e, i, t} = C_{e, i, t} \cdot A_{e, i, t}$ of the non-primary engines $e \ne e^{\star}$ above an activation floor of $25.0$:
   $$B_{\text{orth}, i, t} = 0.15 \cdot \left(\frac{1}{2} \sum_{e \ne e^{\star}} \widetilde{A}_{e, i, t} \cdot \mathbb{I}\!\left(\widetilde{A}_{e, i, t} \ge 25.0\right)\right)$$
3. **Final Composite Score & Actionability (`C-STRAT-04`, `C-STRAT-06`):**
   $$S_{\text{composite}, i, t} = G_{\text{total}, i, t} \cdot \text{clip}\!\left(S_{\max, i, t} + B_{\text{orth}, i, t}, \; 0.0, \; 100.0\right)$$
   $$\text{is\_actionable}_{i, t} \iff \left(G_{\text{total}, i, t} = 1\right) \;\land\; \left(S_{\text{composite}, i, t} \ge 65.0\right)$$
   Candidates are deterministically ranked by $\left(-S_{\text{composite}, i, t}, \; -S_{\max, i, t}, \; \text{ticker}\right)$.
